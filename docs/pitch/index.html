Problem

Your camera roll is a graveyard of intentions.

We screenshot concert flyers, products, books, places and charts meaning to act — then they're buried forever. Nothing turns a screenshot into the next step.

Cloud screen assistants can't touch a work device

"Read my screen" tools ship pixels to someone else's servers. On devices under NDAs, SOC 2 / HIPAA / GDPR duties, that's a non-starter — the useful screenshots are exactly the ones you can't upload.

So the intent dies

No safe path from screenshot → action means people fall back to manual copy-paste, or just forget. The value is stranded on-device.

The six egress rules (egress.py)

1Air-gap: LATENT_OFFLINE=1 blocks everything
2Allow-list host, https, default port
3Body ≤ 4096 bytes (no image fits)
4No base64-like blob (200+ chars)
5PII check finds nothing in the body
6No API key in body (headers only)

Business model

Privacy is the product — and the moat.

B2C — affiliate share

Action cards link to tickets, shopping and books. A planned affiliate.tag_url hook tags those outbound links for a referral share. Free to the user; revenue on intent we already surfaced.

Planned, not shipped — the hook is in open PR #35.

B2B — enterprise tier

The exact thing that blocks cloud assistants is our sale:

  • On-prem / fully local model
  • Policy controls over what may leave
  • Audit-log export for compliance

Same engine, two doors: consumers get convenience, enterprises get provable control.
